The atelier

Four hands.
Six weeks.

i.

The sketch

Every piece starts as a pencil drawing on your hand, so the proportions fit you and nobody else.

ii.

The wax

A wax model is carved and tried on at your second visit. You change anything you like.

iii.

The setting

Stones are set claw by claw under a loupe by one of our four master setters.

iv.

The signature

Each piece is engraved with its number and the setter's initials, then polished by hand for a day.
