# Arise: the cel-shaded Monarch and knights, before and after

Frame captures of the Arise cut on the home page, taken with
`node tools/arise-frames.js capture <dir> cut` (a paused fake clock, so every
frame is exact) at 1280 x 720, then laid out with `node tools/arise-frames.js sheet`.
"before" is main before this change; "after" is this branch. Times are ms from
the cue named in each sheet's title.

- `pose-sheet.jpg` — every pose keyframe drawn on its own: the knight (top row
  without, second row with the greatsword), the two ranks behind, and the Monarch.
- `monarch-rise.jpg` — the word: the Monarch's smear drawings up out of the seal, the squash, the hold.
- `monarch-command.jpg` — the first hit of the drop: the fist pulls to the chest, then the hand snaps out over the army.
- `knights-erupt.jpg`, `knights-erupt-close.jpg` — the drop: each wave erupts hunched, lands low (brace) and snaps upright.
- `salute.jpg`, `salute-close.jpg` — the salute: a dip, the right fist to the chest, the sword up; the Monarch's hand to the sky.
- `kneel.jpg`, `kneel-close.jpg` — the kneel: a breath up, then down hard on one knee, head bowed; the Monarch lowers his hand.
- `title-card.jpg` — the title card and the fade out, unchanged.
